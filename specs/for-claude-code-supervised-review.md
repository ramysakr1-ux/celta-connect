# Supervised review / supervised assignment-writing — naming, contact hours, trainer visibility

Written 15 Aug 2026, for Claude Code.

## Renaming
Any timetable slot previously labelled "Self-study" is renamed. Two specific cases:
- **Review of a prior input session** → "**Supervised review**" (e.g. "Supervised review — phonology").
- **Time set aside to write an assignment** (e.g. the two LFC slots on Tuesday Week 4) → "**LFC assignment writing**" (or the matching assignment name) — never "self-study," even though the trainee works independently, because the session is supervised and submitted.

Never leave a bare "Self-study" label anywhere on the timetable going forward.

## Why this isn't self-study
These slots require the trainee to **submit something** (a short reflection, a quiz, a review task) that the **trainer checks** — that supervision and submission requirement is what justifies counting the slot as contact time, not independent unsupervised study. Any newly-designed review/self-study-shaped session must carry this same submit-and-check structure to qualify for the same naming and hour-counting treatment; a session with no submission stays "self-study" and is not counted as contact time.

## Trainer visibility (the connection to the trainer)
- **Completion flag**: the trainer's queue/dashboard shows a tick (or equivalent flag) per trainee per supervised-review/writing session, indicating it was actually submitted — not just opened.
- **Time spent**: alongside the tick, show how long the trainee spent on the session (tracked automatically while the task is open/active). This gives the trainer a signal beyond pass/fail completion — e.g. a trainee who ticked complete in 90 seconds versus one who spent 20 minutes.
- This applies to **every** slot that gets the "supervised" treatment (reviews and assignment-writing time alike), not just one type.

## Open design question, not yet resolved
Whether the review activity itself is just re-reading material, or re-reading plus a short quiz — leaning toward **reread + quiz for all of them**, but the quiz content/format per session is not designed yet. Flag as a follow-up, not blocking this spec.

# Course modes — what the handbook requires, and what we are missing

Checked against the CELTA Administration Handbook (uploads). Sections cited are the handbook's own.

Three modes, and the thing that defines each is **where teaching practice happens**, not where input happens (3.3):

| Mode | TP | Input | Observation of experienced teachers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Face-to-face | 100% on-site | on-site, or Cambridge Moodle | on-site, face-to-face |
| Online | 100% online live via centre platform | online via Moodle and/or centre platform | online live |
| Mixed-mode | a mix of both | both | both |

Up to three of the six observation hours may be filmed, in every mode.

---

## What we already handle

- Mode set per course and per lesson on the timetable.
- The Stage 3 rule: a borderline Pass/Fail candidate's **final two assessed TPs must be in one mode**.
- Trainer-in-training mode-shadowing (3.7.4), already in `build-spec.md`.
- Mode change mid-course as an ordinary timetable edit, with an emergency path.

## What we are missing — in order of cost

### 1. A TP group may not mix modes at the same stage — 3.5

Verbatim requirement: on mixed-mode courses trainees **must** do TP in both modes, but **a teaching practice group must deliver in only one mode at a time** — not a mix of online and face-to-face TP at the same stage of the course. Candidates teach **one level in one mode and the other level in the other mode** (e.g. upper-intermediate face-to-face, elementary online).

Our model sets mode **per lesson**, which permits a non-compliant timetable to be built without complaint. Mode belongs to **the group's block at a level**, and a timetable that puts two modes inside one group's stage should fail validation, not warn.

This is the expensive one, and it touches the timetable, rotation and the syllabus planning grid.

### 2. Hours per mode are a separate count from the six — 9.1.2

- Mixed-mode: a **3 hour / 3 hour split is desirable**.
- **A minimum of two hours of TP must be scheduled in any one mode.**
- If TP is split into a 2-hour and a 4-hour block, **the 2-hour block is scheduled first**.

The app counts assessed hours toward six. It does not count them per mode, so a mixed-mode course can reach six hours with 5 + 1 and look complete. Add a per-mode counter beside the existing one, with the two-hour floor as a gate.

### 3. Online courses require a demonstration lesson and unassessed TP — 9.1.2

On online courses it is a **requirement** that both a **demonstration lesson taught by a course tutor** and an **opportunity for unassessed TP** are included, to familiarise candidates with the teaching platform. Recommended minimum for the unassessed teaching: **20 minutes of individual teaching**, not team or group.

Neither exists in the design. Both are timetable events with a candidate-visible record, and unassessed TP must be **clearly marked as unassessed** to tutors and candidates alike (the same rule applies to optional extra unsupervised TP on face-to-face courses).

### 4. Observation of experienced teachers must match the mode — 3.3

Face-to-face course → observations of face-to-face teaching. Online → online. Mixed-mode → both. `Observations.dc.html` records the kind (experienced / filmed / peer) but not the mode, so a mixed-mode course cannot show it met both. Add mode to the observation record and surface the split.

### 5. Cross-mode awareness-raising on single-mode courses — 3.5

On 100% face-to-face or 100% online courses it is **recommended** that provision is made for candidates to consider how teaching practice adapts to the other mode — awareness-raising discussion, not teaching.

Consequence for the pre-course supplement: on a face-to-face course, Part A should not simply disappear. It becomes the awareness-raising provision, in a shorter form, and the centre can point an assessor at it.

### 6. Tutors on online and mixed-mode courses must evidence online experience — 3.7.2

All tutors on those courses must be verified **and** able to evidence knowledge and experience of online teaching or training, e.g. having delivered at least one online course. That is a field on the tutor record and a check when a tutor is added to an online or mixed-mode course.

### 7. Moodle input must be augmented, and the assessor told what was used — 3.4

Moodle content was designed for face-to-face teaching. Used on an online or mixed-mode course it **must** be augmented with centre-delivered online input. Assessors must be given **a schedule showing which Moodle sections candidates were asked to complete and what input the centre provided**. That schedule belongs in the assessor pack, and nothing generates it today.

Asynchronous input of any kind must always be combined with live follow-up discussion.

### 8. Course length — 3.6

- Full-time minimum is 20 working days.
- Mixed-mode: centres are **strongly advised to add at least one day** to the 20-day minimum for the mode transition.
- Full-time online and mixed-mode: **strongly advised to extend beyond four weeks** for screen-time breaks and technical contingency.
- A national holiday inside the course dates (not first or last day) allows **one day only** as a study day.

Advice rather than requirement, so a nudge at course setup, not a block.

### 9. Online TP scheduling — 9.1.4

Relevant if the centre runs blocks rather than regular TP: TP should not start until candidates have completed Moodle Units 1–6 (Unit 4 for fully-online full-time); candidates must meet tutors and attend a **TP preparation session** before TP begins; intensive blocks are recommended in the final third but **must not end when the course ends**, so assignments can still be submitted and resubmitted afterwards.

---

## Answer to the original question

**Face-to-face changes almost nothing** for us — it is the mode the design already assumes.

**Mixed-mode is the one that bites**, and it is not the extra day or the tutor experience field. It is that mode is a property of a group's block at a level rather than of a lesson, plus a second hours counter. Everything else on this list is a field, a flag or a document.

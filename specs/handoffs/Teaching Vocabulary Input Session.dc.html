Teaching vocabulary and lexis.
A word isn't one fact to give — it's several: meaning, form, pronunciation, and how it combines with other words. This session works through a real item using techniques you'll use in your own teaching.
Running this session
Push past "look it up in a dictionary" — the point of this session is choosing a clarification technique deliberately, not defaulting to the same one every time.

Chunks, not just single words
Push trainees to teach "take up a hobby" as one chunk, not "take" plus "up" separately — much vocabulary is multi-word by nature.
Don't over-teach
Not every item needs full MFP treatment — a receptive vocabulary item met in a text may only need a quick gloss, not a full clarification stage.
Watch for false friends between techniques and levels
A technique that works cleanly at B1 (a definition) may need a very different one at A1 (realia, mime) — the "right" technique depends on the group.

Connect · TP points library and assignment
Three points per round, three teachers per day. The rotation decides who gets which.
A group of six splits in half — three teach one day, three the next, because three lessons is a day's worth. Your generator already makes exactly three points per TP number. So a candidate's position in the day is which lesson they get, and rotating the order rotates the main aim. That only works if the three points for a round have genuinely different aims — which is the thing to check on the way to publishing, not after.

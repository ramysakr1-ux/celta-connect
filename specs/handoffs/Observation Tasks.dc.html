What this screen is for
A trainer's view across the cohort — who is behind on hours, which task is still unsubmitted. Clicking a candidate expands their own log, the same one they see on their Progress tab. This is not where tasks are marked pass/fail — an observation task has no grade, only "submitted" or "not yet."

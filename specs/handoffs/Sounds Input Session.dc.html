What you're actually learning to teach
Hearing "ship" and "sheep" apart is the easy part — you already can. The job is teaching a student who genuinely can't yet: knowing why they can't, what to do with your mouth to show the difference, and which pairs are worth drilling for their L1. That's what the next two sections are for.

Why it is modelled this way
Because criteria are the unit Cambridge assesses and parts are a centre's scheduling convenience, the app stores one criteria set, one verdict and one resubmission per assignment — and treats parts as an attribute of each criterion rather than a thing with a verdict of its own. That means a centre can stage an assignment, un-stage it, or move a criterion between parts without any of it touching how the assignment is marked or reported.

A grammar reference, always open
Have Practical English Usage or an equivalent to hand — the point isn't memorised knowledge, it's knowing where to check.
Marker sentences must come from somewhere real
Push back on invented, decontextualised sentences — a marker sentence should sound like something a person would plausibly say.
Vocabulary items need the same rigour as grammar
Trainees often shortcut the vocab sheet because it "feels simpler" — a bad clarification technique or missing stress mark is just as damaging as a bad CCQ.

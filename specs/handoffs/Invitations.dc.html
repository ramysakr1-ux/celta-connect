On the colour
A ticked box is ink, not green. Green was doing a job the interface never needed: these are not achievements, they are agreements, and colouring them congratulates someone for reading terms. The only accent on these screens is the small gold dot marking the sentence about expiry — the one line that states a system rule rather than a promise the person is making.

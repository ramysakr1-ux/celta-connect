Receptive skills — terminology
Skimming / Gist — reading or listening fast over a whole text to get the main idea.
Scanning / Specific information — particular facts a reader or listener scans for, ignoring the rest.
Detail — finer points understood only on a closer, slower pass.
Prediction — guessing content from a title, image or context before reading or listening.
Inference — working out something the text or speaker implies but never states directly.
Authentic text — a real text or recording made for real readers or listeners, not simplified for learners.
Correct stage order
Lead-in → Pre-teach vocab → Gist task → Peer check → Detail / specific information → Peer check → Speaking or writing task

# Assessor gate screen — the three terms

From `Certificates and Emails.dc.html`, verbatim. Shown before the pack opens;
`for-claude-code-assessor-visit.md` calls it the "Gate screen (before the pack
opens)" with the button **Agree and open the pack**.

1. I will use this material only to assess this course, and will not copy, share or retain it.
2. I will not attempt to copy, reverse engineer, or reuse how this platform works.
3. I understand this material is confidential to the candidates and the centre.

A fourth condition is stated in the spec's prose rather than as a tick:
*understands access ends when the course closes.* The pack email and the
banner both already say this, so it is information rather than a term.

## Superseded 29 August 2026

Ramy: "not happy with the wording... take a look at the Administration
Handbook and see if there's anything about assessor disclaimer wordings we
can use instead."

**The Handbook has no ready-made assessor declaration.** It is written for
centres, not as terms an assessor ticks, so nothing could be lifted
verbatim. The three below are grounded in the passages that are on point:

1. *I will use this material only to assess this course, and will not copy,
   share or retain it.*
   Unchanged. Matches CELTA 5's own "Cambridge English does not retain
   copies of portfolios."
2. *This material is confidential to the candidates and the centre, and my
   report is confidential to Cambridge.*
   Merges the original term 3 with Admin Handbook 2.4.6: "The assessor's
   report is the property of Cambridge, confidential, and must not be quoted
   from or used for advertising."
3. *My access ends when the course closes.*
   Promotes the fourth condition above from prose to a term.

**Dropped:** "I will not attempt to copy, reverse engineer, or reuse how
this platform works." A software licence clause put to a visiting Cambridge
assessor before they can do their job. No Cambridge document asks it, and it
reads oddly to the professional being asked.

Also relevant if this is revisited, from the CELTA 5's own Candidate
Portfolio section: "Assessors scrutinise a selection of portfolios to
moderate candidates' work on the course, and to agree provisional and final
recommended results", and "The centre stores portfolios and provides
Cambridge English assessors and moderators with viewing rights via a link to
the folders."

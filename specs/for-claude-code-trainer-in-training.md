# Trainer-in-Training (TinT) — full spec

Written 14 Aug 2026, for Claude Code. Repo: `ramysakr1-ux/celta-connect` @ `main`. Source: `Trainer in Training.dc.html`, screens 1a–1d. Cross-checked against the real Cambridge CELTA Trainer-in-Training Handbook (2023, v6) and the CELTA Administration Handbook (2022) — the TinT Handbook is the more recent source and wins wherever the two conflict.

## Core principle: the TinT has no official capacity with candidates
A trainer-in-training (TinT) is a tutor undergoing training who is simultaneously being assessed themselves. They run input sessions, supervise TP, and privately draft feedback like any tutor — but **the only thing they deliver to candidates directly, officially, is an input session.** No document a candidate relies on ever carries the TinT's signature. Every one of those documents is the supervisor's alone.

## Screen 1a — Their workspace (portfolio overview)
Header: TinT's name, "external scheme" or "internal scheme," verification date, supervisor's name. **Verification must precede training** — training done without prior Cambridge verification is never acknowledged, so the app should refuse to open a TinT record with no verification date.

Headline stats: % of input observed, % of TP/feedback observed (both must reach 80% minimum, tracked separately since input can be up to 10% asynchronous but TP/feedback never can be), feedback-countersignature progress.

**Portfolio checklist** (all required in the e-portfolio; this is Route One: Training and Development programme, most common route):
- 8 pre-course tasks (tracking own development, Syllabus & Assessment Guidelines, Administration Handbook, syllabus & course timetable, candidate reading, candidate selection, observing interviews, standards of assessment)
- Input sessions observed, min 80%
- TP and feedback observed, min 80%, both TP groups, both tutors
- **Pre-session handouts for two existing sessions** (Task Twelve Stage 1) — sessions taught by other tutors, TinT designs prep material only, not the session itself
- **Minimum four input sessions delivered** (Task Twelve Stage 2) — **fully self-designed by the TinT, never reused from the centre's own Resource Hub library**, each observed by the supervisor
- TP feedback given — a minimum count of feedback sessions conducted, each observed
- Two candidates followed from start to end (not four — confirmed against Task Nine), tracked via the Candidate Record CELTA 5 at beginning/middle/end: written work, teaching, peer observation, reflections
- Shadow marking — a sample of assignments marked, always double-marked by the supervisor
- 16-item Task Record (Section 7 of the handbook), each task signed off by both the TinT and supervisor
- Post-course reflective essay, 1,500–2,000 words — **the one compulsory task in the whole programme**, regardless of what else is included
- Portfolio and journal, submitted at close

**Modes trained in**: a TinT is approved only for the mode(s) they trained in. Trained online only → must shadow the equivalent of at least 3 days on a face-to-face course before tutoring face-to-face (not "two weeks at one level" — corrected from an earlier draft). Same rule in reverse for face-to-face-only training before online/mixed-mode. Training on a mixed-mode course with equal coverage of both modes (TP, input, feedback) qualifies for both.

## Screen 1a2 — Delivering an input session (Task Twelve)
Every self-designed session the TinT delivers produces two documents, both filed in the e-portfolio:
1. **The TinT's own self-evaluation** — written after delivery, reflecting on strengths and what to develop.
2. **The supervisor's written feedback on that specific session.**

Neither is countersigned, dual-signed, or shown to candidates — this is training evidence between the TinT and supervisor only. Distinct from the ordinary candidate feedback loop on input sessions (Resource Hub), which is a separate, unrelated document.

## Screen 1a4 — Their own working copy, before anything reaches the trainee (Task Thirteen)
Before any TP feedback is finalized, there's a private preparation stage:
1. Check the lesson plan — discussed with the supervisor *before* the lesson.
2. Check the materials/handouts against the plan's stated aims.
3. Observe the lesson, then **draft their own assessment privately** — before seeing the supervisor's view, not after.
4. Discuss the draft with the supervisor before anything is written up as the real, trainee-facing document.

This working copy — draft plus the supervisor's tracked edits and discussion — **never reaches the candidate** and carries no signature at all. It exists purely as portfolio evidence of how the TinT's judgement developed.

## Screen 1b — What actually reaches the trainee
**One signature only, and it is always the supervisor's.** The TinT has no official capacity with candidates, so nothing bearing their name or requiring their input ever reaches a trainee's record. The document a candidate receives is the supervisor's finished, signed feedback — informed by the TinT's private draft (1a4) but never co-authored or dual-signed in a way visible to the candidate. If the supervisor substantially rewrites the TinT's draft, both versions (the TinT's original and the supervisor's edits) stay together in the TinT's e-portfolio, for the assessor to read later — but only ever one clean document, signed by the supervisor alone, exists in the candidate's file.

## Screen 1b2 — Feedback on how they gave feedback (Task Thirteen)
A separate, private document between the TinT and supervisor: not what the feedback said, but how the TinT delivered it — eliciting vs. telling, prioritising action points, handling a defensive reaction, pacing, tact. Discussed straight after each feedback session the TinT conducts, filed in the e-portfolio, never reaching the candidate.

## Screen 1c — The extra assessor day (external scheme only, Handbook 13.7 / Admin Handbook 3.7.4)
Required only when: the TinT trains on the External scheme, or trains on the Internal scheme at a centre other than the one nominating them. On this day, the assessor:
1. Reads the TinT's e-portfolio, alone.
2. Observes the TinT conducting an input session.
3. Observes the TinT supervising TP.
4. Observes the TinT conducting a TP feedback session.
5. Discusses the training programme with the TinT and separately with the supervisor.

Afterwards, the assessor completes the **CELTA Trainer-in-Training Assessor Moderation Report** and sends it to the JCA for the nominating centre, who forwards it to Cambridge English Nominations (per the 2023 TinT Handbook — supersedes an older, differently-named form in the 2022 Admin Handbook). Connect prepares the day and the portfolio; it never produces or holds this report. Booking note: the extra day must be arranged when the course assessment itself is booked, not discovered afterwards — a real, forgettable cost.

## Screen 1d — Outcomes, and the internal-scheme alternative
**Three possible outcomes** at the end of training: confirmed as Assistant Course Tutor (not yet Main Course Tutor — that needs ≥2 further courses as ACT plus shadowing an MCT); training extended; or not verified (rare, always discussed with the TinT and supervisor first).

**Rules not tied to a specific screen:**
- One TinT per course is recommended; a centre wanting two on one course should contact their JCA first.
- The TinT must work a minimum of three further courses at the nominating centre after training, so the competencies gained are actually used.
- On the **Internal scheme**, screen 1c doesn't exist — the supervisor alone assesses the TinT's work and e-portfolio, and sends their own moderation report straight to the JCA. Everything else (portfolio, private drafts, feedback-on-feedback, input session self-evaluation) is identical.
- Supervisor time commitment, for centre staffing: ~5 hours pre-course, ~1 hour/day during, 8–10 hours reviewing tasks across the course, ~5 hours post-course.

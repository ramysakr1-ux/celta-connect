What "grades" would look like

Grounded in the real Word document, C13/2026 provisionals — a plain dropdown, not Connect's propose-then-MCT-confirms flow, since Lite has no MCT/tutor distinction to hang that on. Two values a tutor picks, riding in the same tracker record Stage 1/2/3 already use — no new store or backend plumbing.

"On the left you have the provisional grade, comments referencing the criteria, strengths and action points in planning and teaching. After the assessor meeting we fill out the part on the right — that's the final."

Only the two grade values travel into Lite. The strengths, action points, and the write-up behind them stay exactly where they are now — nothing about that document moves.
